## What YEO can help with

- **Your credential:** getting one, and renewing it before it expires.
- **Android package access:** the private repository address and your login.
- **Your integration:** setup, recommended flows and problems you cannot solve from these docs.
- **Going live:** questions before your release, or an evaluation on your target devices.
- **Several people on one installation:** how to handle identity on your platform.

## What to include

To help us answer quickly, tell us:

- the SDK or plugin version, and the platform;
- the phone model and operating system version;
- what you expected, and what happened;
- any error code or message;
- whether it also happens on an unshielded build, if your app uses a shielding product.

!!! warning "Keep sensitive data out of support requests"
    Never send face images, gallery files, your credential or your repository login.
